Microphone Access
SumSnap uses your microphone to hear numbers as you speak them — so you can add values hands-free without typing.
🔒 Audio is processed on-device only. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
⚠️ Browser may block mic & camera
This file is open as file:// which most browsers treat as insecure. For mic & camera to work, serve it locally:

Open a terminal in this folder and run:
python3 -m http.server 8080
Then open: http://localhost:8080/SumSnap_Offline.html
